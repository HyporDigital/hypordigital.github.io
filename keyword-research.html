HyporDigital Keyword Research

HyporDigital Keyword Research is our internal app for measuring search demand. It shows how many people search for a brand's products, its category and its competitors, so our campaign plans and recommendations start from real numbers.

What it does

The app connects to the Google Ads API and retrieves keyword ideas and historical search statistics from Google Ads Keyword Planner: average monthly searches, month-by-month trends, competition and top-of-page bid ranges. We use those figures in brand research, keyword strategy and campaign planning for our clients.

Who uses it

The app is used only by the HyporDigital team, signed in with Google accounts that have access to our own Google Ads account. It isn't offered to the public and there's no sign-up.

What it can and can't do

It only reads keyword planning data. It doesn't create, change, pause or delete campaigns, and it doesn't spend money.

Privacy

How the app handles Google user data is described in our privacy policy, including how to remove its access. Questions go to service@hypordigital.com.
