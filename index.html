Free brand analysis

See your brand the way your market sees it

Send us your brand name and an email address. We'll research how you show up online, from search and reviews to ads and competitors, and send you a graded report with the three things we'd fix first.

What's in your report

  • A scorecard across 11 growth areas

    Search demand, Google rankings, marketplace rankings, website SEO, page speed, site quality, reviews, ad reach, checkout and trust, AI search visibility and brand consistency. Each one is graded A to D against a stated standard, so you can see exactly what was measured.

  • How you stack up against your rivals

    Wherever we can measure them, your numbers sit next to the brands you compete with, so a grade comes with context, like 2nd of 5 on search demand, or 3 of 4 rivals ahead of you on page speed.

  • Your advertising, mapped

    Which ad platforms you're on, the creative and offers running now, how deep you discount, and how often you email and publish. You'll see where the gaps are before a competitor finds them.

  • Three priorities to start with

    The changes most likely to grow revenue, picked across every area and checked against the data before they reach you. Each one says what to do and why.

How we work

From first look to steady growth

Every engagement starts with research, so the plan is built on what's actually happening in your market.

  1. 1

    Research

    We audit your brand from the outside: your site, ads, reviews, competitors and how many people search for what you sell.

  2. 2

    Plan

    You get a focused plan with the changes most likely to grow revenue, what each one costs and how we'll measure it.

  3. 3

    Build and run

    We build the campaigns, pages, systems and automations, launch them and manage them day to day.

  4. 4

    Measure and improve

    Reporting is tied to revenue and reviewed with you on a set schedule. What works gets more budget and what doesn't gets cut.
